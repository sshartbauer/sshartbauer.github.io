Tools & Apps

Small tools built for clinical learning and academic study. Each one started as a personal need and grew from there.

Live
Clinical Language
Hospital Spanish Phrase Guide
A clinical communication reference organized by situation: assessment, vitals, movement, consent, discharge, and emergency. Full phrases are written in informal tu form with pronunciation guides, built for students preparing for clinical rotations or working in multilingual care environments.

Academic Study
Organic Chemistry Flashcard App
An interactive study tool for organic chemistry concepts, reactions, and mechanisms. Built to make the visual and pattern-recognition side of orgo more accessible, especially for students without a strong prior chemistry background.
Open tool → Live
Academic Study
Organic Chemistry Reaction Game
A companion to the flashcards: a visual reaction-tree game where you start from a molecule and pick the right reagents to navigate forward through synthesis pathways. Built to drill the pattern-recognition side of mechanisms — Markovnikov vs anti-Markovnikov, oxidation states, leaving groups — in a low-stakes way.

Research
JumpstartGEN: General Research Tool
A research assistant built for first-generation college students tackling their first academic papers. Enter a research question and get optimized search strings, relevant peer-reviewed papers, methodology guidance, variable identification, dataset recommendations, and alternative framings of your question. Designed to give you a structured head start without doing the thinking for you.
