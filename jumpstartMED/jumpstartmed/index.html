JumpstartMED is a planning aid. AI output can be wrong, so check every dataset, guideline, and claim against the original source. It does not replace your institution's IRB, a research mentor, or a medical librarian. Paper records come live from PubMed and registered studies from ClinicalTrials.gov. Evidence labels and study design notes are general teaching points, and your IRB decides what counts as human subjects research at your school.
