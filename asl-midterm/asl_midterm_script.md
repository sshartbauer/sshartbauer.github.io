# ASL Midterm Story: College/Job — Gloss Script (~150 signs)

Notes:
- CAPS = sign gloss. [bracket] = facial/body cue (non-manual marker, NMM).
- Hyphenated words (GIVE-UP) = one sign, two English words.
- "wh-q" = wh-question face (brow furrow, head tilt). "rhet-q" = rhetorical question face (brow raise, head tilt, pause, then answer).

---

## Part 1: The Job (present the setup)

PAST 11-YEAR, ME WORK SECURITY. [neutral, establish topic]
COMPANY BIG, PLACE SPECIAL, RULES STRICT. [serious]
ME LEAD. TEAM, PEOPLE 30, ME OVERSEE. [brow raise on LEAD, proud]
JOB ME RESPONSIBLE MANY THING. [nod]
GOOD JOB. BUT... ME HEART WANT DIFFERENT. [shift face, softer, wistful]

## Part 2: The Turn (why-q, motivation)

WHY ME CHANGE? [wh-q]
ME WANT BECOME DOCTOR. [determined, brow raise]
SO, ME DECIDE, SCHOOL START AGAIN. [head nod, resolve]

## Part 3: Why ASL / Why Doctor (the heart of it)

WHY DOCTOR, WHY ME? [rhet-q, pause]
ME FAMILY, ONE PERSON DEAF. [gentle]
BEFORE, THAT PERSON LIP-READ, STRUGGLE. [furrowed, empathy]
ME WANT COMMUNICATE DIRECT, EASY, RESPECT. [sincere, warm]
THAT WHY ME LEARN ASL, YOU-ALL, CLASS HERE. [nod toward camera/class]
ALSO, MEDICINE, ME WANT HELP PATIENT, EVERY-DAY. [warm, open]

## Part 4: College Life

COLLEGE NAME LMC, PLACE ANTIOCH. [neutral]
ME STUDY BIOLOGY, SCIENCE. [matter-of-fact]
CLASS: CHEMISTRY, PHYSICS, MATH, ALL HARD. [brow furrow]
SOMETIMES ME STRUGGLE, CONFUSE, TIRED. [tired face, sigh]
BUT ME GIVE-UP? NO! ME KEEP-GOING. [strong shake head, determined]

## Part 5: Balancing Job + School

EVERY-DAY, ME LIFE: MORNING WORK, AFTERNOON CLASS, NIGHT STUDY. [list with space/timeline, matter-of-fact]
BALANCE HARD, TIME LIMITED. [head tilt, exhale]
FAMILY HELP ME: HUSBAND, TWO SON, SUPPORT. [warm smile]
THEY PROUD ME, SO ME PROUD ME-SELF TOO. [smile, chin up]

## Part 6: Future Goal

FUTURE, ME PLAN: FINISH LMC, TRANSFER UNIVERSITY. [hopeful]
FIRST-CHOICE BERKELEY. IF NOT, BACKUP DAVIS. [neutral, shrug]
AFTER THAT, MEDICAL SCHOOL, BECOME DOCTOR. [determined, eyes forward]
ME WANT WORK HOSPITAL, HELP PEOPLE EVERY-DAY. [warm, hopeful]

## Part 7: Closing

ROAD LONG, HARD. BUT ME READY. [strong nod, confident]
ME NEVER GIVE-UP. [firm]
FINISH. [neutral, small nod to close]

---

**Rough sign count: ~148-155** depending on how you count listed items (e.g. CHEMISTRY, PHYSICS, MATH counted individually). This lands right in your comfort zone for slow signing within the 2-5 min window.

**Biggest grading lever:** Communication (10 pts) rewards the facial cues above, not just correct handshapes. Practice the face changes as deliberately as the signs themselves — especially wh-q on "WHY DOCTOR," the softer face on the deaf family member line, and the resolve on "GIVE-UP? NO!"
